Line items are allocated per bill line, so they can differ from the totals by about ₹1 per bill. Finance › Register Summary takes advances settled off one step earlier: its "collection before tax" is this collection less advances settled, and its "Including Tax" equals "Received at the desk" here.

All billing figures come from live bills · up to now, today included.

Collection (excl. tax)
Money that came in against these bills with GST taken out. It excludes value cards and packages the client had already paid for, and it includes amounts settled from advances the client deposited earlier, because an advance is money. This is what the monthly target is measured on.
Tax collected
The GST inside that collection. Collection excl. tax plus tax collected is exactly total collection (a bill's rounding paise, when there are any, are shown as their own line).
Total collection
Money in against these bills including GST. The sum of the two tiles before it.
Redemptions
The part of what was sold that the client paid from value cards and service balances bought earlier. Not money received in this period — it arrived when the card or package was sold.
Sales value
Everything sold at full value before tax — services, products, value cards, memberships, advances deposited — including the part a client paid from a value card or package. The top line of the Finance register. Shown in the reconciliation card rather than as a tile.
Received at the desk
Total collection, less the part settled from earlier advances. This is the register's "Total received" — cash, card, UPI and other payments.
Monthly target
Collection excl. tax for the month against the target set under Admin › Centers. "On pace" projects the month-to-date figure over the whole month. Note this differs from the Finance register's "collection before tax", which also excludes advances settled from earlier deposits.
Refunds
Shown in the reconciliation card and not deducted from any tile. Refunds on cancelled bills are excluded because the bill itself is already out of every figure.
Previous period
For a month to date, the same days of the previous month. For any other range, the equal-length window immediately before it.
Day of the week
Average sales for each weekday across the period. A day the salon was closed counts as zero.
